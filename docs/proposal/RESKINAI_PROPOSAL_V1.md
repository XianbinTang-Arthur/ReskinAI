# ReSkin AI Proposal (V1)

Updated: 2026-02-12  
Stage: 0-1 validation (pre-seed / commercialization preparation)

## 1. Project Definition

### 1.1 One-liner
ReSkin AI is an AI-assisted tattoo concept co-creation platform for people with visible scars, helping them move from passive scar burden to active identity expression in a private, emotionally safer workflow.

### 1.2 Problem we solve
- After medical survival, many people still face long-term body image anxiety, social withdrawal, and reduced self-confidence.
- Existing options are fragmented: medical scar treatment focuses on physical repair, while generic tattoo inspiration tools are not scar-aware.
- The hardest gap is the "I want to do something, but I am not ready yet" stage. Users need low-pressure exploration before speaking to an artist.

### 1.3 Beachhead users
- Primary segment: women with visible post-surgical scars who are open to tattoo-based identity restoration.
- Secondary segment: burn and trauma survivors with visible scars.
- Key stakeholders: scar-experienced tattoo artists/studios, survivor communities, mental health-aware partners.

## 2. Product Strategy

### 2.1 Product boundary (non-medical)
- ReSkin AI is not a diagnosis or treatment tool.
- Output is concept design guidance; final execution decisions are made by users and professional tattoo artists.

### 2.2 MVP scope (must-have first release)
1. User side
- Upload scar photo with clear consent and privacy controls.
- Enter style preferences (motifs, meaning, mood, avoid-list).
- Generate multiple scar-aware tattoo concepts.
- Save, compare, and rate concepts with feedback tags.

2. Professional side
- Artist view for user-approved concepts and preference notes.
- Artist feedback panel for feasibility and adaptation.
- Version history for co-creation traceability.

3. Safety and governance
- Emotional safety notice and easy opt-out in flow.
- Unsafe content filtering (self-harm encouragement, hate, violence prompts).
- One-click deletion of user data and generated assets.

### 2.3 Unique value
- `Scar-aware`: design logic centers scar shape/region context, not generic text-to-image output.
- `Emotion-aware`: user-controlled pacing, low-judgment experience, privacy-first interaction.
- `Co-creation`: bridges users and artists to improve real-world execution quality.

## 3. Core Assumptions and Validation

### 3.1 Key assumptions
- H1: Users are willing to upload scar images if privacy controls are explicit.
- H2: AI concepting reduces first-consultation anxiety.
- H3: Artists adopt the tool if it improves consultation efficiency.
- H4: Users value expression and identity agency over concealment-only framing.
- H5: The workflow can support sustainable willingness to pay.

### 3.2 8-week validation plan
1. Participants
- 30 target users.
- 10 tattoo artists with relevant scar coverage experience.

2. Program
- Weeks 1-2: low-fidelity flow interviews and risk mapping.
- Weeks 3-5: interactive prototype usability testing.
- Weeks 6-8: pilot user-artist closed-loop workflow testing.

3. Go/no-go thresholds
- End-to-end flow completion >= 65%.
- Self-reported consultation anxiety reduction >= 60%.
- Artist rating "useful as pre-consultation tool" >= 70%.
- Data permission/deletion workflow success = 100%.

## 4. Business Model and Sustainability

### 4.1 Revenue design
1. B2C
- Single concept pack: USD 9-19.
- Premium multi-iteration pack: USD 29-49.

2. B2B studios
- Studio SaaS subscription: USD 39-99 per month.
- Team collaboration seats (later stage).

3. Partnership revenue
- Co-programs with survivor organizations.
- Training collaborations on scar-aware design workflows.

### 4.2 Cost structure
- Model inference and secure image storage.
- Product engineering and UX iteration.
- Community operations and support.
- Privacy/compliance controls and audits.

### 4.3 Unit economics goals (to be validated)
- Reduce CAC by 30% within 6 months.
- Reach 8-12% paid conversion in early cohorts.
- Keep studio monthly churn below 8%.

## 5. Go-to-Market Plan

### 5.1 Entry strategy
- Community-first, then scale: launch through survivor communities and specialist artist networks.
- Story-first, not ad-first: rely on trust, referrals, and authorized transformation stories.

### 5.2 First 90 days
1. Days 1-30
- Onboard 5 seed artists.
- Conduct 20 deep interviews/usability sessions with target users.

2. Days 31-60
- Launch closed MVP pilot.
- Ship baseline consent, retention, and deletion workflows.

3. Days 61-90
- Complete >= 15 user-artist closed-loop cases.
- Publish 3 consented case stories for community trust building.

## 6. Impact and Measurement

### 6.1 Intended impact
- Increase confidence and body agency in scar-bearing users.
- Reduce anxiety linked to visible scars.
- Improve communication quality between users and tattoo artists.

### 6.2 Measurement framework
1. User outcome metrics
- Pre/post confidence score change.
- Pre/post anxiety score change.
- Body acceptance perception change.

2. Product metrics
- Completion rate, return rate, paid conversion.
- Time-to-first-useful-concept.

3. Ecosystem metrics
- Active artist partners.
- User-to-artist handoff conversion.
- Community partner retention.

## 7. Risks and Mitigations

### 7.1 Primary risks
- Emotional trigger risk during scar visualization.
- Sensitive data/privacy risk from scar imagery.
- Aesthetic bias risk in generated concepts.
- Regulatory messaging risk if perceived as medical claim.

### 7.2 Mitigations
- Trauma-aware UX, skip/exit controls, support resource prompts.
- Data minimization, explicit retention windows, one-click delete.
- Diverse training/evaluation samples across skin tones and cultures.
- Consistent non-medical disclaimer across product and communications.
