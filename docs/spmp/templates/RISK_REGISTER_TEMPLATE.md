## Scoring Guidance

- Score = Likelihood x Impact
- 1-5: Low
- 6-10: Medium
- 11-15: High
- 16-25: Critical

## Escalation Guidance

- High: review mitigation within 24 hours.
- Critical: immediate escalation and action plan.
- Privacy/safety events: follow SCMP incident workflow.
